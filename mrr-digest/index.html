For Solo & Small-Team SaaS Founders on Stripe

Your MRR and churn, every week, in your inbox. No dashboard to log into.

MRR Digest connects to Stripe and sends you a short weekly email with your MRR, churn, and key changes — nothing to set up beyond connecting your account.

The problem

Tracking MRR, churn, and signups usually means checking a dashboard, exporting Stripe data, or building your own spreadsheet. Most founders don't need another tab to check — they need the numbers to just show up.

The solution

Zero-setup

Connect Stripe once. No dashboard, no login habit to build — the digest comes to you.

Weekly email

A short, readable summary of MRR, churn, and notable changes — not a wall of charts.
