For Solo Consultants, Coaches & Freelance Providers

Your inbox, triaged and drafted for you — you just hit approve.

An AI agent that reads your inbox, flags what's urgent, drafts replies in your voice, and escalates anything it's not confident about. You stay in control; it does the grinding.

The problem

Manual email triage and reply-drafting eats 1–2 hours a day for solo operators without admin support — time that should go to client work, not inbox management.

How it works

1. Triage

Connects to your inbox and sorts incoming email by urgency and topic automatically.

2. Draft

Writes a reply in your voice for routine messages — you review and approve with one click.

3. Escalate

Anything ambiguous or sensitive gets flagged for you directly instead of a risky auto-send.

Why this, not just a filter tool

Most inbox tools sort and file email. This is built around the triage → draft → escalate workflow specifically, so ambiguous or high-stakes messages don't get auto-answered — they land in front of you with context, ready for a quick decision.
